Your files never touch remote servers. All processing executes inside your browser memory via WebAssembly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are images uploaded to any cloud servers?

No. All recipe automations run entirely inside your browser using WebAssembly. Your photos never leave your device.

Can I process multiple images at once?

Yes. You can drag and drop up to 50 photos into the batch queue for simultaneous recipe execution.
